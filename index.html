    One round from start to crash

    Every round in chicken roads follows a simple cycle. A betting window opens, players set a stake, and then the multiplier starts climbing. The longer the round continues, the faster the risk grows. At a random point set before the round begins, the flight ends and the crash happens. Anyone who cashed out before that moment keeps the multiplier linked to their stake; anyone still in the round loses that stake.
